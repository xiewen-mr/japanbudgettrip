Affiliate Disclosure

Japan Budget Trip may use affiliate links in the future. If you click an affiliate link and make a booking or purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

Editorial independence

Affiliate relationships do not control our editorial opinions. We aim to explain practical tradeoffs and only place links where they are relevant to the topic, such as hotels, transport, eSIMs, pocket WiFi, activities, and travel insurance.

How links are chosen

Resource links should support a planning decision already explained on the page. A link is more useful when it helps readers compare final price, cancellation terms, route fit, coverage, availability, or official rules. A higher commission should not be used as the reason to recommend a worse fit.

No paid placement by default

Japan Budget Trip does not currently sell sponsored rankings. If a page later includes a sponsored placement, it should be clearly labeled so readers can distinguish it from normal editorial guidance.

Reader responsibility

Prices, availability, refund rules, pass eligibility, insurance terms, and provider policies can change. Always review the final terms on the provider's website before booking or purchasing.
